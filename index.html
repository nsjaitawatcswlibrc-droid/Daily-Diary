Access On Your Phone & Other Devices

You can use DayTrack from your mobile phone, tablet, or work laptop. Your handwritten stylus notes and daily activities work seamlessly on any touch screen.

Instant (Local Wi-Fi)

1. Connect on Your Home / Office Wi-Fi

When your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network, start a quick local server on your PC and open the IP address in your phone's browser:

npx serve . -p 3000 or python -m http.server 3000

Then on your phone, open http://<YOUR_PC_IP>:3000 (e.g. http://192.168.1.15:3000).

Anywhere in the World

2. Access Anywhere via Free Web Hosting

You can upload this project for free to GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify. You'll get your own secure URL (e.g. https://my-diary.vercel.app) accessible 24/7 from any phone or computer.

  • Works on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, and Mac.
  • Add to Phone Home Screen as an App (PWA).
  • Handwrite with Apple Pencil, S-Pen, or finger touch.
